# Captions

These English SRT and WebVTT subtitle files accompany the Book Genesis 5 launch videos.

Their cue boundaries are approximate, prepared from the recorded narration text, scene frame durations, and narration durations in `src/timing.json` in the editable Remotion project. They are not automatic-speech-recognition output. Cues preserve the wording spoken in the rendered narration, with short clause-based line breaks for readability.

- `book-genesis-5-long.en.srt` and `.vtt`: 59.9-second landscape walkthrough.
- `book-genesis-5-short.en.srt` and `.vtt`: 32.8-second vertical cut.

The small extension of some final cues to a scene boundary keeps captions continuous through the visual transition; it does not add spoken copy.
