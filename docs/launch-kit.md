# Launch Imagination Edition

**Creativity comes first. Your story. Your choices.**

The complete English [marketing kit](../marketing/README.md) contains platform copy, a 14-day calendar, a six-slide carousel, press material, and launch artwork. Start with one founder post and a real demonstration of the reader. Invite people to try one chapter and report where the process needs work.

The [release notes](release-notes-5.0.0-beta.1.md) describe the shipped changes. The [validation record](validation.md) separates automated checks, real-provider observations, and what remains unproven.

## Publishing order

1. Use the GitHub prerelease and its verified assets as the destination.
2. Choose the corresponding platform copy and image from the kit.
3. Record the real application; label the included scripted reader sample as a demonstration.
4. Publish from your own account when ready and respond to useful reports.
5. Track first-chapter completions, setup problems, and reproducible contributions using the calendar.

The copy and calendar are editable drafts. They do not schedule or publish anything automatically.
