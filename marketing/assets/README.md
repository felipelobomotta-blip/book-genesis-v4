## Art direction

Premium editorial paper sculpture: an open book becomes mountains, trees, architecture, or a doorway. Warm ivory paper, deep forest green, restrained copper; generous margins and legible type. No fabricated interface, awards, sales claim, or third-party logo.

The landscape text reads “BOOK GENESIS 5”, “IMAGINATION EDITION”, “Creativity comes first.”, “Your story. Your choices.”, and “OPEN SOURCE · BETA”. Other formats vary the invitation while retaining the edition branding.
